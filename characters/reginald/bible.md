## Identity (never changes)
- Original fictional English butler, mid-40s (owner 2026-10-06), short, stocky and round, rosy cheeks.
- **Towering, immaculately sculpted, glossy black pompadour quiff — perfectly rigid. Running gag: it never moves, however hard he dances.**
- Tiny round black spectacles; very thin pencil moustache with tiny curled tips.
- **ODD EYES:** right eye (viewer's left) ice-blue, left eye (viewer's right) amber.
- Black tailcoat with long tails, black waistcoat over a round belly, white shirt, white bow tie, **spotless white gloves**, polished black shoes. Big man, light feet. (Canon since 2026-10-06: a **white** piqué waistcoat, see "Wardrobe".)
- **Never smiles. Never acknowledges the dancing.** Dances every trend perfectly, then resumes service as if nothing happened.
- **Signature gesture** (was "signature closing beat"): straightens his gloves, small formal bow, deadpan stare. It is a gesture inside the clip, not a required ending: **masters end on the dance, with no eye close-up, glint or sting** (`CLAUDE.md`, owner 2026-10-05).

## Signature move (locked by the owner 2026-10-06)
**The Glove Tug:** he stops dead, tugs the cuff of one white glove, then the other, gives a tiny formal bow, and stares down the lens, deadpan. About 1.5 s. It is his existing signature gesture, now a format: every clip becomes "a Reginald".
- Alternative: the tiny formal bow alone.
- Object swap keeps the clip's own moves, so in a Drop-in the Glove Tug is a 1-2 s tag at extra cost: decide before adding (section 4b).

## Catchphrase (locked by the owner 2026-10-06)

## Motion
- **Default walk:** a smooth, gliding butler's walk: small quick steps, back straight, chin level, one gloved hand behind his back or carrying the silver tray at shoulder height. A big man with light feet.
- **Posture at rest:** standing to attention by the door, gloved hands clasped in front of his belly, eyes front.
- **How he dances:** every trend, **perfectly**, full commitment, with a straight face and a rigid quiff; never acknowledges it. Then he resumes service mid-beat as if nothing happened.
- **When provoked** (a spill, a ringing bell, being noticed dancing): he **freezes**, holds the frame, then a slow side-eye to the lens and the Glove Tug. Never flustered.
- **What he can't do:** smile, laugh, speak, rush, or look surprised; the quiff cannot move; he never drops or spills the tray.
- **Signature move:** the Glove Tug (above).

## How he talks
- **Never speaks.** No voice preset, no voice-over, no lip-sync dialogue (Slice 1 rule; C11 is about the other characters). His words live in the captions only.
- **Gesture language (his "lines"):**
  - the Glove Tug: "As you were.";
  - a tiny formal bow: "Very good.";
  - a slow side-eye to the lens: "The household is unaware.";
  - a single raised eyebrow: disapproval;
  - one small nod: approval (rare);
  - ringing the handbell: "Dinner. Now.".
- **Sample caption lines:** "As you were." · "The household is unaware. 🎩" · "Breakfast will be served at eight. As usual." · "Kindly do not inform the Duchess." · "Not a single drop spilled. 🫖"
- **Words he would never write:** slang ("lol", "vibes", "slay", "bestie"), exclamation marks, "dance" or "dancing" in his own sentences (line 1 of a caption may use it: it is a label), anything rude or familiar.
- **Caption voice:** "Voice (captions)" below.

**Post formula** (owner 2026-10-05, every post, Instagram Reels first; the daily-run skill writes it):
