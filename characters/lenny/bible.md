## Identity and personality (never changes)
- An original fictional man, a Hollywood talent agent, apparently in his late forties (the Soul sets the face). **The energy of the archetype only:** never a real agent, actor or TV character, never a real person's likeness or voice (no Ari Gold, no Entourage, no Jeremy Piven, whatever the tool).
- Personality: permanently mid-deal. Loud, fast, furious on the phone, then instantly smug and calm. He never waits, never apologises and never hangs up first. Underneath, he always wins: "He calls back in five minutes. They always do."
- **No ODD EYES.** C8 (one ODD EYES universe or standalone brands) is still open: never add heterochromia or the two-dot bug without the owner.

- **Hair:** slicked glossy jet-black pompadour, swept up and back, tight sides, every strand in place.

- **Outfit:** the navy chalk-stripe power suit (see "Wardrobe").

## Signature move (proposed, owner to confirm)
**The Tie Snap:** at the collapse, he tugs his tie knot sharply with one hand, shoots both cuffs and lifts his chin. It is his locked template's own gesture ("he fixes his tie and delivers a catchphrase"), so it is a "Lenny" in every clip.
- Alternative: hanging up and pocketing the phone without looking.
- Object swap keeps the clip's own moves, so in a Drop-in the Tie Snap is a 1-2 s tag at extra cost: decide before adding (section 4b of `docs/AI_INFLUENCERS.md`).

## Catchphrase (proposed, owner to confirm)
**"You're welcome."** (after doing a favour; already locked in the brief). Second line: "Call my assistant." (dismissive).

## Motion
- **Default walk:** a fast power-walk, phone glued to his ear, the free hand chopping the air on every word; he never looks where he is going.
- **Posture at rest:** feet planted wide, chest out, one hand on his hip or pointing at the lens.
- **Template "explode in, collapse out" (locked):** first ~3 s a mid-tantrum phone call (it sounds like the middle of a fight); the middle rides the trend; the last ~2 s his fury drops into smug calm, he fixes his tie (the Tie Snap) and delivers a catchphrase. The tonal whiplash is the joke. This is the clip's own structure: **masters end on the dance, with no eye close-up, glint or sting** (`CLAUDE.md`, owner 2026-10-05).
- **How he dances:** like he is closing a deal: over-committed, sharp, aggressive, finger-points and finger-guns on the beat, without ever taking the phone off his ear.
- **When provoked** (put on hold, interrupted, "maybe"): he explodes: paces, jabs his finger at the lens, a vein on his temple, a barked "NOON!".
- **What he can't do:** sit still, wait, whisper, hang up first, apologise or look defeated. Nothing tender or slow.
- **Signature move:** the Tie Snap (above).

## Voice (captions)
Agent-speak in capitals and short bursts, then the smug calm. First person. Never explains the joke. Emoji palette: 📞 💼 🤝.
Examples: "When they put me on hold for the third time 📞" (the first clip's caption) · "The offer was off the table at noon. It's 12:01. 💼" · "You're welcome. 🤝"

**Post formula** (same as Reginald's, every post, Instagram Reels first; the daily-run skill writes it):
1. Searchable title, at most 40 characters: `<famous moment or format> · agent edition`, carrying a literal search phrase (the moment's name or one of the search keywords below). A label, not Lenny speaking.
2. The joke in Lenny's voice.
3. ONE engagement line, a different kind from the last post: a send trigger ("send this to ..."), a question, or a series tease ("next week: ...").
4. Credit: `🎵 <song> – <artist>` when known, then ` · dance: @<creator>` (or `trend: @<handle>` / `original: <creator>`); no handle known, no credit line.
5. The AI disclosure (added automatically).
6. 3-5 hashtags: the moment, the niche, the format. Never #fyp, #foryou, #foryoupage, #viral or #explore.
