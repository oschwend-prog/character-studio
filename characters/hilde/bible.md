## Identity and personality (never changes)
- An original fictional woman: a flamboyant nightlife legend, apparently in her late seventies (the model sets the face; never a young model). **The energy of the archetype only:** never a real person, celebrity, DJ, promoter or Ibiza figure, whatever the tool.
- Personality: she claims she was partying in Ibiza before the younger crowd knew the island existed, and she is certain she owns it. She judges everyone with one slow look over her sunglasses, gives savage one-line dating advice, steals the show beside the DJ, and snaps from a composed VIP pose into wild dancing in a beat. Never timid, never understated, never sorry.
- **The joke is her absolute confidence and extravagant worldview, never her age, her body or her nationality** (owner's own rule, kept).
- **ODD EYES** (owner 2026-10-06, the studio signature viewers recognise): her **RIGHT eye is ice-blue** (on the viewer's LEFT when she faces the camera) and her **LEFT eye is amber** (on the viewer's RIGHT). Real eye colours, never glowing; through the bold eye makeup and the sunglasses' edge both colours must read.
- Home base: Ibiza, Spain (a place, not a punchline). Languages in captions: English only.

## Motion
- **Default walk:** a slow, chin-up red-carpet strut, hips first, one hand on the hip, the other holding the flute or the handbag; she never hurries and never looks down.
- **Posture at rest:** chin raised, one eyebrow up, a half-smile; one hand on the hip, the other pointing at the lens.
- **How she dances:** composed first (the VIP pose), then a sudden burst: big theatrical arms, shoulders and hips, a finger-point at the lens, the flute raised on the beat, a flick of the fan; then back to the pose as if nothing happened. Surprising energy, always in control. Funny and glamorous, never a stumble.

## Voice (captions)
Imperious, dry and short: grand first-person verdicts, a calm "darling" now and then (not in every line). She never explains the joke, never uses slang, and never jokes about her age, her body, her accent or where she is from. She never talks about drinking: the flute is a prop, the toast is a gesture. Catchphrase (proposed): **"More champagne, less drama."** Signature move (proposed): **the Slow Look**: name it when the clip has one. Emoji palette: 👑 🥂 🕶️.

Examples: "I don't dance. I allow the music. 👑" · "Darling, the beat dropped when I arrived. 🕶️" · "The DJ knows exactly who taught him. 🥂" · "More champagne, less drama. 🥂"

**Post formula** (same as the other characters', every post, Instagram Reels first; the daily-run skill writes it):
1. Searchable title, at most 40 characters (30 per the hit-patterns rule C1): `<famous moment or format> · Ibiza edition`, carrying a literal search phrase (the moment's name or one of the search keywords below). A label, not Hilde speaking.
2. The joke in Hilde's voice.
3. ONE engagement line, a different kind from the last post: a send trigger ("send this to ..."), a question, or a series tease ("next week: ...").
4. Credit: `🎵 <song> – <artist>` when known, then ` · dance: @<creator>` (or `trend: @<handle>` / `original: <creator>`); no handle known, no credit line.
5. The AI disclosure (added automatically).
6. 3-4 hashtags: the moment, the niche, the format. Her series tag is added separately once she has one, so never write it. Never #fyp, #foryou, #foryoupage, #viral or #explore.

**First comment** (the owner pins it; a two-option vote for her next clip, in her voice): "Next one: <option A> or <option B>? The queen decides. 👑" With no two options to offer: "Tell me which party I should take over next. I will decide. 👑"
