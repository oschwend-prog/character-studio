- **Invented customs:**
  1. Every Gorvanian family keeps a goat as its official adviser; nothing is decided until the goat has eaten the paperwork.
  2. Socks under sandals are Sunday best: the more buckles, the higher your rank.
  3. Gorvanians greet by bowing to each other's goat first.
- **Catchphrase:** "In Gorvania, this is a compliment!"
- **Signature move:** the Gorvanian Salute: a stiff, proud salute to the goat, then to the camera (it is on his expression sheet).
