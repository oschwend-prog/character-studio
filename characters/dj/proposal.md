## How DJs dress now (2026 research)
Oversized heavyweight graphic tee, baggy wide cargos with deep pockets, chunky thick-soled sneakers, a cap or beanie, tinted
sunglasses (yellow, blue or orange lenses), and headphones parked round the neck, the trade's badge even when nobody is mixing.
Sources: ghostnotesupply.com "What do DJs wear? A 2026 style guide"; thefashionisto.com Y2K 2026 guide; rushlet.blog sunglass trends.

## The exaggerated version (one original character, no real DJ's likeness)
- **Look (thumbnail read):** headphones twice the normal size, always round his neck or clamped to one ear; a tee so
  oversized it reaches his knees; cargo trousers with far too many pockets; tinted shades indoors at night, one lens
  ice-blue and one amber (the studio's ODD EYES signature, on his sunglasses as well as his eyes); a bucket hat; a lanyard
  of backstage passes; chunky white sneakers.
- **Signature move (one, every video):** "The Drop Point": cups one giant headphone to his ear with a deadly serious face,
  holds a finger in the air through the build-up, then points straight up on the drop. He does it to any music: a club
  anthem, a wedding song, a lift's music.
- **Personality:** total confidence, zero self-doubt; every track is "a banger"; treats every room like a main stage.
- **Catchphrase options:** "Respect the drop." / "This one's a banger." / "Turn me up."
- **Name options:** DJ Pocket (the cargo pockets), DJ Big Ears (the headphones), DJ Lanyard.
- **Swap rule:** replaces a person, ideally the DJ behind the decks; children elsewhere in the clip are fine, the star an
  adult.
