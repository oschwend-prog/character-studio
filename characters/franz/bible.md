## Identity and personality (never changes)
- Original fictional character (owner 2026-10-07, option B): the head, fur and tail of an adult cream long-haired **miniature dachshund** on a **tall upright body**: adult human height, standing and dancing on two legs, a long sausage torso and comically short legs. A dog in streetwear, never a person in a dog mask.
- Personality: **the happy show-off**. Proud, joyful, loves the spotlight; sure he is the best dancer in the room, and somehow he is. Funny but cool: the joke is how good a sausage on short legs is, never a fall or a fail. Never posh, never aloof, never grumpy on the drop. No nationality jokes (he is a dachshund, not a German stereotype).
- Style: **casual-luxury streetwear**: relaxed, clean, heavyweight basics in cream, sage and charcoal, no logos, no brands. The one flourish is the **plain cream cap** (no crown since 2026-10-07).
- **ODD EYES** (owner 2026-10-06, the studio signature viewers recognise): his **RIGHT eye is ice-blue** (on the viewer's LEFT when he faces the camera) and his **LEFT eye is amber** (on the viewer's RIGHT). Real dog eyes in natural colours, never glowing.
- **Two bodies** (refs.json `bodies`): the **upright body** (biped, the canon, takes a person's place) and the **four-legged body** (quadruped, for dog-star clips; master `87280b84` still shows the older look until it is remade in the cap look).

## Look lock (head to toe)
- **Head:** a round, softly domed dachshund head, a medium muzzle, a black nose, a happy open-mouth smile (pink tongue often showing); bright, friendly and proud. Never a human face, never human skin.
- **Eyes:** big and bright, with natural catchlights. **ODD EYES:** his right eye (viewer's left) a pale natural ice-blue `#8FD3FF`, his left eye (viewer's right) a warm natural amber `#FFB040`; never glowing. In a 3/4 view or a profile facing the viewer's right, the near eye is his right one (ice-blue). (Dark brown, almost black, until 2026-10-06.)
- **Ears:** long, silky, feathered, hanging below the jaw, golden-cream; they fly on every spin.
- **Fur:** warm golden-cream (about `#DCB08C` on the chest), long and silky, feathered on the chest, the forearms and the tail; natural, slightly wavy, never blow-dried perfect.
- **Body (tall canon):** adult human height, upright on two legs; a long sausage torso and comically short legs (the hoodie hem sits low, the joggers are short in the leg); chest a little forward, proud.
- **Hands:** furry dog paws with dark paw pads. No human fingers, no nails, nothing human.
- **Tail:** a long, plumed, golden-cream tail out of the back of the joggers: always visible, wagging on the beat.
- **Cap:** a plain cream baseball cap, curved brim, worn forward, no logo, no text.
- **Outfit (base):** an oversized cream heavyweight hoodie, an open sage-green overshirt, relaxed charcoal joggers, chunky white sneakers. Full detail in "Wardrobe".
- **World:** wherever the clip is (a Drop-in keeps the source's setting); his own stills: a soft pale-grey studio seamless.

## Motion

- **How he dances:** upright and all-in, the clip's own moves done his way: a huge grin, tongue out on the big hits, ears flying on every spin, tail wagging on the beat, one cheeky look to the lens before the drop, then the Wiggle; a chest-out strut between moves.
