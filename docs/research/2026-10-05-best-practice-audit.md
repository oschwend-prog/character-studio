# ODD EYES Character Studio: best-practice audit (short-form growth and analysis)

**Date:** 5 Oct 2026. **Author:** analyst agent (read-only on the repo; this file is its only output).
**Scope:** TikTok + Instagram Reels practice as of October 2026, checked against the spec, the launch docs, the playbook, the bibles, `config/scan.json`, the two skills, the code in `studio/`, and every installed skill and MCP tool that bears on social work.
**Excluded:** everything in `final-fix-wave.md` (M-1 to M-13, B1 to B7). Where a gap touches one of those items, it is cross-referenced and not re-reported.
**Evidence labels:**
- **[P]** primary source: platform docs, an official newsroom, or the platform's own code/API reference.
- **[S]** secondary source: an industry blog or trade press.
- **[U]** unverified: sources conflict, or the only source is a blog with no primary citation.
- **[>12m]** the source is more than 12 months old.

No credits were spent. Only free tool *descriptions* were loaded. Nothing was posted, uploaded or connected.

---

## (1) Verdict

1. **The production and safety engineering is top decile; distribution is not.** Idempotent posting, budget caps and the kill switch, enforced AI disclosure, pre-registered bars on medians, the no-scrape and no-third-party-audio rules, and clean masters to both platforms are already ahead of most creator operations.
2. **The distribution layer around each post is missing:**
   - no first-hour community work and no pinned comment;
   - no custom covers;
   - no search keywords in the hook text or the caption;
   - Instagram Collab, `audio_name` and Trial Reels are not used, although Postiz already supports Collab and Trial Reels;
   - in-app trending sounds have **no posting path**, yet three week-1/2 concepts depend on them.
3. **The learning loop is half-blind.** Follower counts, follows, TikTok watch time and completion, and Instagram non-follower reach are **never collected**. So two things cannot work:
   - the binding "mechanism floors", which must be fixed before week 3;
   - the Instagram originality guard, which reads `non_follower_pct`. Nothing ever writes that field.
4. **Drop-in is the biggest policy exposure.** It means our character swapped into someone else's viral footage, at a 70% TikTok / 40% Instagram default share. Three things stack against it:
   - Instagram's 30 Apr 2026 aggregator rule;
   - TikTok's "unoriginal" rule;
   - unknown rights in the Higgsfield library clips.

   The guard meant to catch trouble has no data.
5. **Monetisation will come from brand deals, not platform funds.** TikTok Creator Rewards is effectively closed: it needs videos of 60 s or more, and fully-AI content is reportedly ineligible. The route is brand deals, marketplaces and IP. None of the cheap groundwork exists yet: a business contact, a trademark, branded-content plumbing, a media kit.

   **Overall: about 60% of best practice.** About 2 weeks of mostly S/M work would get to about 85%.

## (6) Already at or above best practice (keep)

- **Creative fundamentals:**
  - a move on frame 0 and seamless loops;
  - 10–15 s masters and 6–8 s eye loops;
  - one signature end beat (the eye glint + sting) that doubles as the loop seam and the brand;
  - a consistent named character instead of anonymous template clips;
  - series, lore and comment prompts designed in (playbook §1.4/§2.4/§4.6).
- **Research discipline:** vidIQ outliers normalised by creator median; "≥ 2 creators before calling a pattern"; the Viral Picks rubric with sub-scores, a brand-safety gate and analyst reasons; evidence labels throughout the playbook.
- **Measurement discipline (stronger than typical creator analytics):**
  - KPI bars pre-registered and binding;
  - medians, never means;
  - n ≥ 5 before a rule, and a "Watching" list below that;
  - `outlier_x` against a rolling 15-post baseline, which controls for account growth;
  - missing data never counted as 0.
- **Compliance:**
  - AI disclosure is always appended and can't be suppressed (M-11);
  - TikTok `is_aigc` is always on;
  - bios say "AI character";
  - no third-party audio;
  - Creator accounts (full music library, marketplace-eligible);
  - one clean master to both platforms (no cross-platform watermark);
  - ≤ 5 hashtags (Instagram's cap);
  - no scraping;
  - untrusted tool output is treated as data.
- **Operations:**
  - approve-first, with autopilot only after 6 approved posts;
  - idempotent claims with `needs_check` on uncertain publishes;
  - ≤ 2 posts per account per day;
  - a hard credit cap and a kill switch;
  - timezone-correct slots;
  - evidence-based posting times (Sprout UK / Buffer) and a cadence ramp (3 then 5 a week).
- **Risk choices:**
  - the 1852 Outsider premise, which avoids the "national origin" line;
  - no meme coins and no undisclosed realism;
  - an anatomy and smile QA on every clip.
