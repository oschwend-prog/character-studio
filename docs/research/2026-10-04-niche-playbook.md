# ODD EYES — niche playbook (TikTok + Instagram Reels), 4 Oct 2026

**Scope:** what is winning right now (posts from 5 Aug – 4 Oct 2026) for our three AI-character lanes — **Biscuit** (dancing dachshund), **Reginald "the Quiff"** (deadpan butler), **The Outsider** (fish-out-of-water talker, not yet designed) — and how ODD EYES should play the algorithms.

**Evidence labels:** **[E]** = measured / sourced (vidIQ outlier data or a cited source). **[I]** = my inference or recommendation. "Outlier ×" = the post's views ÷ that creator's median views, as reported by vidIQ.

**Method**
- 6 × vidIQ `instagram_tiktok_outlier_search` (30 credits; balance 88 → 58). Filters: posted after 2026-08-05, views ≥ 200K, English captions, one post per creator per platform, 12 per platform. 144 outlier slots analysed.

## 0. The ten moves that matter most

1. **Build every clip as a loop, with the move already happening in frame 0.** 19 of 24 V1 outliers were seamless loops and 21 of 24 were music-only [E]. TikTok says finishing a video is a "strong indicator", and Mosseri says the first seconds decide whether a Reel gets wider distribution [E].
2. **The Higgsfield Genjutsu animal-dance format is already proven and still new.** @bellvtrix.ai's "Made with Higgsfield Genjutsu" two-cat dance has 17.4M views (44.6×). @muduronline's dog-in-front-of-dancers clip has 2M at 997× with 707 followers [E]. Genjutsu only launched on 1 Sep 2026 [E], so we are early, not late [I].
3. **The current pet meme is "Single Ladies": a dog front and centre with human dancers behind.** Four outliers between 16× and 997× [E]. Biscuit should lead it this month, in a better version than the one-click CapCut template [I].
4. **Reginald's best template is the false-premise skit, not the plain dance.** A text premise, a mundane task done with total seriousness, then the dance. @eatfryhaven's "first day as an assassin" got 43.4M views (1,393×), the biggest outlier in all six scans. A barista "favourite song" POV got 5.6M (3,475×) [E].
5. **Silent characters with a distinctive silhouette are breaking out right now.** Jean Phil (AI, one action, changing locations) went from 0 to 5.6M views on his second TikTok within days of 17 Sep 2026 [E]. An English copycat, "Archibald Brown", had 110K followers in about 5 days [E]. Reginald's quiff is exactly this kind of asset [I].
6. **Those same characters were exposed fast.** They were undisclosed and tied to meme coins. An AI-detection account's exposé of Jean Phil got 1.6M views [E]. ODD EYES stays openly AI and never touches tokens [I].
7. **The Outsider should be the 1852 Victorian gentleman, not the invented-kingdom prince.** Comedy about being foreign in time is proven: AI history "POV vlogs" reach 20M+ views per video [E]. It also avoids TikTok's "national origin" hate-speech line, which an accented foreign prince keeps brushing against [E/I].
8. **Use numbered series to win follows.** "EP.30", "Episode 6", "Part 2 / Previously…" posts score 13–236× [E]. TikTok tends to recommend the next part to viewers of the last one [E, practitioner source].
9. **Labels are now required.** On 31 Aug 2026 Instagram said profiles featuring an AI-generated person that lack the "AI-generated profile" label lose recommendations [E]. Turn it on for Reginald and the Outsider, and label Biscuit too. The EU AI Act's Article 50 disclosure duty has applied since 2 Aug 2026 [E].
10. **Post 4–5 times a week per character from week 3, around 19:00 UK, Tue–Thu first.** That slot covers the UK evening peak and US East Coast early afternoon. Going from 1 to 2–5 posts a week gave +17% views per post, and 11+ a week gave +34% [E: Buffer, 11.4M posts].

### 1.2 Hooks, length, structure, audio, captions, hashtags

**First-second hook patterns [E→I]**
- In the outliers the hook is visual first: the animal is already mid-move or staring down the lens at frame 0, and the beat drops on frame 0. When text appears, it is an attitude line or a relatable POV, never a description.
- Patterns to use, each ≤ 30 characters:
  - Ego claim: `he hits every single beat` · `lead dancer. obviously.`
  - Myth-bust: `they said sausages can't dance`
  - Self-aware twist: `forgot I'm a dog for a sec`
  - Relatable POV: `me at the vet acting fine`
  - Job framing: `hired as the office DJ`
  - Signature: `eyes don't match. moves do.`
  - Challenge: `be honest. rate this /10`
- Rule [I]: the first move must land within 0.3 s. Never open on an establishing shot.

**Length [E]:** TikTok outliers in V1/V2 ran 5–20 s (median about 15 s). Under-7 s videos average about 32% completion versus about 11% for 10–15 s (practitioner dataset).
→ [I] Run two lengths: **10–15 s dance loops** as the main product, and **6–8 s eye loops** as the rewatch engine. Socialinsider's "45–60 s Reels get the most views" finding is from business accounts, not character entertainment, so it does not apply here.

**Loop vs story:** loop. 19 of 24 V1 outliers loop [E]. Make the last frame match the first frame: end on the eye stare, cut back to the same stare [I].

**Audio:** 21 of 24 V1 outliers were music-only [E]. Use a **trending in-app sound** whenever the clip rides a named trend ("Single Ladies", "Be OK"). Otherwise use our own AI beat. That keeps the clip clean of third-party audio and builds a recognisable "Biscuit beat" [I]. Never reuse a driver's audio (spike finding).

**Caption style:** lowercase ego plus one emoji. The first line is the joke, not a description. The second line, when needed, is a comment prompt. Examples are in the concepts table.

**Hashtags (Instagram hard cap is 5 since Dec 2025; TikTok 3–5) [E]:**
`#dachshund #sausagedog #dogsoftiktok #dancingdog #aicharacter`
- Rotate `#doxie` / `#weeniedog` in for `#sausagedog` on US-leaning posts.
- On Instagram, swap `#dogsoftiktok` for `#dogsofinstagram`.
- Tool tags (`#higgsfieldgenjutsu`) pull in an AI-creator audience rather than pet lovers. Test them on at most 1 post in 5 [I].

### 1.4 Series and comment drivers (follower conversion)

- **"You pick the prop"** (weekly). The pinned comment asks what Biscuit wears next, and the next clip is a **reply-with-video** to the top comment, so the comment shows as a sticker. That closes the loop: comment → video → new comments [E: practitioner source; I: series design].
- **"Day N of learning every trend"**. Numbered series are what the V6 outliers have in common (EP.30 at 13.7×; "Part 2" at 236×) [E].
- **"Good morning" daily loop**: a habit format, with the same framing every morning [I].
- **Comment prompts that work without begging** (TikTok makes "like for like" bait ineligible for the For You feed [E]):
  - rate /10
  - find the missed beat
  - which eye is watching you
  - what should he wear next
- **Pin**: intro clip · eyes clip · first breakout.

### 1.5 What to avoid

- **Undisclosed realism.** A real-looking dog doing human choreography without a label invites "is this real?" exposés. Instagram and TikTok both detect C2PA metadata and watermarks [E]. Label every post [I].
- **Hands moving the dog's paws.** That is the real-pet version of the format; @cashewtokk got only 3.8× [E], so it lifts accounts less, and it is not our lane anyway [I].
- **The one-click "Single Ladies" photo template** (CapCut). It saturates fast and looks cheap. Only do Single Ladies with full-body skilled choreography, and drop the trend by about 20 Oct [I].
- **Uncanny anatomy.** The AI dancer "Benjamin Stachio" drew 59K comments, mostly "this is not a human shape" [E]. Keep Biscuit's dachshund proportions on biped drivers, and QA ears, tail and coat continuity [I].
- **Third-party footage or audio in the output, branded costumes, an exposed belly.** These are bible rules and remain correct.
- **Long AI pet narratives** in Slice 1: a different cost structure and an unproven product for us [I].

- **Late trends.** Trends burn out fast. NewEngen tagged three trends from the week of 15 Sep "saturation risk" in that same week [E]. Rule [I]: ride a trend within 7 days of its peak, or skip it.
- **"Look what AI can do" framing** (R8). It attracts the wrong audience, and 52% of users say they disengage when they suspect low-effort AI [E, practitioner survey; treat as directional].
- **Undisclosed AI human.** Instagram makes unlabelled AI-person profiles non-recommendable (31 Aug 2026) [E].
- **Tokens, coins, "collabs" with crypto.** This is what turned Jean Phil and Archibald Brown into exposé fodder [E].

### 3.3 Hooks, length, structure, audio, captions, hashtags

- **Hook:** a premise caption on frame 0 (all-caps or sentence case, ≤ 30 chars), and he speaks the first line within 0.5 s.
  - Spoken first lines that work in O1: "So I'm told…", "Right. Explain this to me."
  - Speak the keyword: TikTok indexes speech (ASR) and on-screen text (OCR) for search [E]. For example, say "flat white" out loud.
- **Length:** 15–25 s for one joke plus one tag line. The O1 trend runs 30–60 s, but it uses stitched multi-clip vlogs [E]. Start short (cost per second of AI voice and video) and move to 30–45 s only for proven episodes [I].
- **Structure:** a story, not a loop.
  - Premise → misread → escalation → button line (catchphrase).
  - End on his eyes and the catchphrase so the replay restarts cleanly [I].
- **Audio:**
  - His AI voice, plus light period-flavoured underscore (harpsichord / music-hall) as an audio signature [I].
  - Burned-in word-synced captions (O1 convention) [E].
  - Never clone a real person's voice; AI voice-clone disputes are live (the Tsuda/TikTok AI-voice case, Sep 2026) [E].
- **Caption:** his voice, one sentence, an outraged understatement: "I requested it flat. It arrived round."
- **Hashtags (5):** `#timetraveller #victorian #londonlife #charactercomedy #aicharacter`. Add `#historytok` on history-heavy episodes [E: the O1 hashtag pattern includes #history #historytok #pov #ai].

### 3.6 What to avoid

- **National, ethnic or religious targets, accents, broken English and pseudo-languages.** TikTok's hate policy protects national origin, and satire must be clearly framed as satire [E].

### 4.2 AI labels, suppression and exposure: what happened to others

**Platform rules now [E]**
- **TikTok:**
  - Labels realistic AI. More than 3B videos labelled as of 10 Jul 2026, using C2PA, invisible watermarks and creator labels.
  - Says labelled AI stays eligible for the For You feed.
  - Since Nov 2025, viewers can turn AI content down with a "Manage Topics" slider. **The label works as an audience filter** [E/I].
  - US For You feed retrained on US-only data under the Oracle-led joint venture (from 22 Jan 2026). Expect reach swings and "distinctly American" trends [E].
- **Instagram:**
  - Post-level "AI info" label: Meta says no algorithmic reach penalty.
  - The account-level label, renamed **"AI-generated profile" on 31 Aug 2026**, is required for profiles featuring an AI-generated person, or the profile becomes non-recommendable.

### 5.2 Outcome metrics (pull at 1 h, 24 h, 72 h, 7 d)

- **Primary per-post score:** `outlier_x = views_7d ÷ rolling median views_7d of the account's last 15 posts` (the same logic as vidIQ, so it is comparable to the scans). A **hit** is outlier_x ≥ 3 [I].
- **Mechanism metrics:**
  - average watch time
  - **watch ratio** = average watch time ÷ length; > 1.0 means loops
  - completion %
  - 3-second retention (TikTok) / skip rate (Reels)
  - shares/sends per 1K views
  - saves per 1K
  - comments per 1K
  - likes per 1K
  - **follows per 1K views** (series value)
  - profile visits
  - % non-follower reach
  - % search traffic (TikTok)
- **Account-level:** followers, follows from crossovers (spikes on the partner account within 24 h of a Collab).

### 5.3 Weekly review protocol (Monday) [I]

1. Compute outlier_x and the mechanism metrics for every post from the last 7 days.
2. **Feature lift table:** for each tag value with **≥ 5 posts**, compute the median outlier_x vs the account median. Report only lifts that hold on both platforms or are explained by a platform difference. A single post never sets a rule.
3. **Pre-registered bars (set before week 3, not renegotiated after seeing data):**
   - keep a format if median outlier_x ≥ 1.5 over ≥ 5 posts
   - kill it if ≤ 0.7 over ≥ 8 posts
   - a hook pattern is "proven" at ≥ 2 hits in 10 uses
4. **A/B discipline:** use Trial Reels for hook variants (same clip, two hooks) and the audio arm (trending vs own beat) on alternating posts. Change one variable per test.
5. Output: next week's format mix, for example 60% proven / 30% challenger / 10% wild-card. Retire one loser and add one new outlier format from the scans.
6. Log the lessons (`ecc:growth-log`). Each week, recheck the policy and label status for the three accounts.
