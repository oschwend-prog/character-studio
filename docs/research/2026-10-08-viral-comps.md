# ODD EYES: viral comps, Jean Phil and Abu Shalab (8 Oct 2026)

**What this is:** a study of the two AI characters the owner named as comps (owner, 8 Oct): **Jean Phil**
(`@jean_philanthrope`) and **Abu Shalab** (`@abu.shalab`). It covers who they are, how they post, what gets them their
millions, how they make money, and what ODD EYES should copy or avoid. It builds on `2026-10-07-hit-patterns.md` §0 and
`docs/launch/measurement-and-learning-plan.md` and does not repeat them. The JeanPhil design rules
(`docs/AI_INFLUENCERS.md` §4b) are checked against a second case here.

**Labels:** **[E]** = evidence (the source is named). **[I]** = my inference.
**Source tags:** **(LC)** = the social-data MCP (LunarCrush), queried 8 Oct 2026. **(TT)** = the public TikTok profile
page, read on 8 Oct. **(WEB)** = a named article (listed under Sources). **(X)** = a post on X as returned by LC.

**Limits (read first):**
- **LunarCrush is still in "limited data mode".** Every Instagram lookup returned an error or nothing, so **every
  Instagram figure here comes from articles or the accounts' own claims**, not from a measurement.
- LC reports "engagements" (its own total, which may include views), not views. Its follower counts were stale (e.g.
  Jean Phil 158K on TikTok in LC, 266.9K on the live page).
- I made **22 LC calls; 10 returned nothing** (an error, an empty list or the rate limit of 4 calls a minute).
- Instagram and TikTok pages are behind a login: **I could not see comments, pinned or first comments, Stories or
  carousels.** Socialblade refused the request. Nothing was downloaded or watched; I read only text.
- vidIQ and ScrapeCreators were not used (as instructed).

## 0. Summary in 10 lines

1. **Both are fully AI characters, and both are made the way we make Drop-ins**: real phone footage with the person replaced by one fixed character. The technology is not their edge; the casting and the cadence are. [E (WEB) vendors; I]
2. **Size on 8 Oct:** Jean Phil has 266.9K TikTok followers (TT) and about 238K on Instagram (6 Oct, WEB). Abu Shalab has 409.1K on TikTok (TT) and about 510K on Instagram (5-6 Oct, WEB). Both were built in about two weeks. [E]
3. **One look and one action, in a new place every time.** Jean Phil shadowboxes in a houndstooth suit around Paris. Abu Shalab dances in a white thobe. A bowl-cut or bob haircut and a moustache make each one readable at thumbnail size. [E (WEB)]
4. **The clip is a two-beat skit:** something ordinary first (an espresso, oysters, chasing a cricket with a sandal), then the signature move. [E (WEB, LC)]
5. **Captions are 2-7 words, written by the character.** There is a catchphrase ("Oui Madame" is in 2 of Jean Phil's 5 top TikToks) or a false brag ("Throwback to when i won red bull dance battle" is Abu's biggest post, 24M engagements). There is no trend label. [E (LC)]
6. **Cadence:** Jean Phil posted daily for 6 days, then slowed, and his TikTok engagement fell 90% from its peak. Abu posted several clips a day: about 25 Instagram posts in about 10 days. His engagement is still at about half of its peak. [E (LC, WEB)]
7. **Reels and TikTok only, as far as can be seen.** Each account also has an X account. Growth was multiplied by other people's remixes, green-screen cut-outs, CapCut templates and "real or AI?" exposés, which they did not make themselves. [E (LC)]
8. **The money is meme coins, not brand deals:** $JEANPHIL ($12.2M, then $2M) and $ABU (bio: "Official $ABU"). Both personas pose as real people and do not disclose that they are AI. [E (WEB, TT, X)]
9. **For us, in order of impact:** (1) make every clip the character's own two-beat bit; (2) a 10-day launch burst for one character; (3) short in-character captions (a false-brag arm for Franz); (4) a rivalry between our own characters; (5) let viewers remix our clips. [I]
10. **Never copy:** coins or tokens, posing as a real person, a real culture's or religion's dress as the joke (Abu), another creator's famous performance passed off as ours, or a Jean Phil-type look. [E/I]

## 3. Format and style

**Length and quality.**
- About 10 s per clip. The how-to that copies the method works with a 10 s clip, and its tools run 4-30 s. [E (WEB UlazAI, a vendor); I for the comps themselves]
- Photoreal, **phone-shot real footage** in real places: Paris streets, a museum, a café terrace, an oyster bar, an apartment (Jean Phil); a stage, a dance battle, streets, Dubai (Abu). [E (WEB KYM, LC)]
- Vendors that copy the method name Seedance-based "Motion Recast" at 720p, Abacus AI with GPT Image 2.5, and Higgsfield. The prompt is "replace the person in {video} with this character {pic}. keep the same motion, color grading and camera quality". **These are sales claims.** [E (WEB, LC); unverified]
- [I] The render is good enough that the exposés came from context rather than artefacts: "no online presence before a few days ago" (Reddit, WEB KYM). Resolution is not the bar. Believable real footage is.

**Structure (the two-beat skit).** Each clip has an ordinary setup, then the signature move. [E (WEB KYM; LC descriptions by @memescreens)]
- Jean Phil: drinks an espresso with a croissant, then stands and boxes; eats oysters, then shadowboxes; shadowboxes in front of an old-master painting in a museum; shadowboxes in the street to French rap.
- Abu Shalab: creeps up on a cricket with a raised sandal, then the beat drops and he dances; on a stage with "abgehackten, fast roboterartigen" (choppy, almost robot-like) moves [E (LC, @ricogotti86)]; sways to "Be OK" (Ingrid Michaelson), the same trend as our playbook's R5 [E (LC, @green.scream.memes)]; a real dance-battle clip captioned as his "Red Bull" win.
- **Hook:** [I] the first frame is the look. The face, hair and outfit are the "what is this?" moment. The payoff (the move) lands within a few seconds. This is consistent with hit rule 1 (pays off by second 3).

**Recurring elements.**
- Same face, hair and outfit in every clip; only the place changes (Abu: only the outfit varies, per his site) [E (WEB)].
- No numbered series, no episode cards. Rivalries and crossovers came from the copycats, not from the originals [E (WEB Les Gens d'Internet, LC)].

**Captions (TikTok, LC).**
- Jean Phil: "Jab, crochet. T'as senti le danger" (17.9M); "She feels safe with the man" (12.4M); "Double G. Oui" (9.5M); "Do you always dress this sharp? Oui Madame" (8.7M); "I see the sun shine at night. Oui Madame" (6.1M). French and English mixed; the catchphrase **"Oui Madame"** is in 2 of the 5. [E (LC)]
- Abu Shalab: "Throwback to when i won red bull dance battle" (24M); "Welcome to the party" (6.5M); "A pump might be fun" (6.1M, five days before his coin launched on pump.fun); "With my new friend" (2.3M). [E (LC)]
- No hashtags and no trend name in any caption LC returned. [E (LC)]
- On-screen text could not be checked (no video was watched). [gap]

**Sound.** French rap (Jean Phil's street clip); "Be OK" and the battle's own music (Abu). No voice: **both are silent characters.** [E (WEB, LC)]

**Language and audience.** Remixes and posts about them came in Arabic, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Russian and English [E (LC)]. [I] A silent character with a 3-word caption travels across languages.

## 5. What gets them millions

**Outliers against the typical post.**
- **Jean Phil's posts are evenly strong.** His 5 visible TikToks run from 6.1M to 17.9M; LC gives an average of 6.48M per post, so the best is about 2.8× the average. [E (LC); I: the character was the hit, not any single clip]
- **Abu's results are skewed.** His first post, real dance-battle footage with a false-brag caption, got 24M, about 3.7× his next best (6.5M). On Instagram, his biggest Reel (57-58.7M) is about 30% of his ~183-200M total views over ~25 Reels. [E (LC, WEB); I for the ratios]
- **The first post was the biggest for both.** [E (LC)] [I] A strong first impression, posted when the account was brand new, got the widest test from the platform.

**What drove the reach** [E (LC, WEB); I where marked]:
1. **Novelty of the look.** A face people can't place. "Is Jean Phil real or AI?" (2.3M engagements, @carterpcs).
2. **Other people's remixes.** Green-screen cut-outs and CapCut templates (Abu's "Be OK" cut-out), "AI overlay" clips, duets, and copycat characters tagging the original. Fans copied the haircut ("vou deixar meu cabelo igual o do Abu": "I'll get my hair like Abu's").
3. **Controversy.** Jean Phil's second peak came the day after an exposé (1.6M views, WEB KYM). The person in Abu's battle footage, or a rival riding the meme, answered with "says he won Red Bull but I beat him two years in a row" (883K and 540K engagements, LC, @abu_harja). [I: correction-bait drives comments]
4. **Coin promoters.** Dozens of X posts push both coins and link back to the accounts. [I: some of the reach was bought or coordinated; it cannot be measured]

**Comments and the first comment:** I could not read them (login wall). From secondary reports, the comment themes were
"the next internet star", then "bamboozled" once people learned he was AI [E (WEB YPulse)]; "real or AI?"; and repeating the
catchphrase [I]. **Whether either account uses a pinned or first comment is unknown.** Our first-comment vote (hit rule 8,
Test F) stays as it is.

## 7. Lessons for ODD EYES (ranked by expected impact)

Each lesson gives the change, the evidence behind it and how to test it. **All are [I]; none changes a binding rule.**
Owner decisions are marked.

### L1. Each character gets one two-beat bit, and every clip follows it (highest impact)
- **Evidence:** both comps repeat one setup → payoff unit in a new place each time (§3). Jean Phil's posts are evenly strong (§5): the bit, not the clip, is the hit. It matches our playbook's best Reginald template, the false-premise skit (4 Oct playbook #4).
- **Change:**
  - Add a pick criterion: **"calm, then burst"**, meaning about 1-2 s of an ordinary action before the first big move, with the payoff still by second 3 (hit rule 1).
  - Caption the setup in the character's voice:
    - **Reginald** is in service (a tray, a door), then the trend, then he resumes service.
    - **Franz** struts in like he owns the room, then the Wiggle on the drop.
    - **Lenny** is on the phone mid-deal, then the drop, then the Tie Snap.
  - This is the existing `traits.moves` and the hit-patterns series cards, made the default shape of every clip.
- **Test:** add a tag `structure: setup_payoff | straight_dance` to the 13 fixed tags (measurement plan §2). Compare median `outlier_x` and follows per 1K at 8 or more per arm, pooled.
- **Cost:** none for picks. A 1-2 s signature tag stays an extra-cost owner decision (AI_INFLUENCERS §4b).

### L2. A 10-day launch burst for one character (**owner decision**)
- **Evidence:**
  - Both comps did their growth in the first 10 days, with daily or more frequent posting.
  - Jean Phil's TikTok fell 90% once he slowed down (a 6-day gap). Abu, posting several a day, holds at half his peak.
  - Their first post was their biggest.
  - Confounders: the coins, the exposés and promoters on X.
- **Change:** one character (the next to go live, or Reginald from Mon 12 Oct) posts **1 a day for 10 days**. That stays inside our hard cap of 2 a day per account, with our usual slot.
  - About 10 Drop-ins ≈ 1,000 credits, roughly 550 more than the Tue/Wed/Thu rhythm over the same days.
  - It needs about 10 owner drops for that character (launch phase: production only from drops).
- **Test:** followers gained per day and the median views per post over the 10 days, against the other characters on 3 a week. Report it as a description: with 1 character it is not a controlled test. Jean Phil reached 235K from 8 posts, so volume may matter less than the character.

### L3. Captions: 2-7 words in the character's voice, with a false-brag arm for Franz
- **Evidence:**
  - Every top caption of both comps is a short line in the character's voice, with no trend name and no hashtags.
  - "Oui Madame" repeats.
  - Abu's biggest post pairs famous footage with a false brag ("Throwback to when i won red bull dance battle").
- **Change:**
  - Add **"false brag"** as a hook and caption pattern for **Franz**, the show-off: "Throwback to when I won [the trend]". It fits his persona exactly, and correction-bait invites comments.
  - Put the catchphrase ("Sausage coming through!", "As you were.", Lenny's) into the caption joke line, which §4b already asks for.
- **Test:**
  - Add the false brag as an arm in the hook bandit (Test B).
  - Bring forward the queued caption test, **joke-first against label-first** (hit-patterns C3; measurement plan §3.7 "next in line"). The comps are a second data point for joke-first.
  - Hit rule 7 (trend name in line 1) stays until that test reads.

### L4. A rivalry between our own characters (already planned; more evidence for it)
- **Evidence:**
  - The wave grew through rivalries (Archibald Brown as Jean Phil's British rival) and crossovers.
  - Two copycat crossovers got 6.1M and 3.9M engagements on accounts with 74.8K and 456 followers (LC).
  - We own all three characters, so the rivalry costs us no outside risk.
- **Change:** run the planned weekly crossover (channel-strategy: at most 1 per character per week, as an Instagram Collab) as a **named rivalry**:
  - Franz against Reginald for "best dancer in the house";
  - Lenny trying to sign Franz.
  - It needs two-performer source clips (Genjutsu takes up to 8 reference images).
- **Test:** Collab posts against solo posts on `outlier_x` and follows per 1K (format tag `crossover`).

### L5. Make our clips easy to remix
- **Evidence:** a large share of both comps' reach came from other people's green-screen cut-outs, templates, duets and "AI overlay" clips (§5). The originals did not make any of them.
- **Change:**
  - Keep Remix (IG) and Duet and Stitch (TikTok) **on** for every post; check that Postiz does not switch them off.
  - Later: one silent green-screen cut of the signature move a month.
  - Our two-dot bug and ODD EYES mark every remix as ours.
- **Test:** count remixes and duets per post in the weekly review (by hand, free).
- **Risk:** others will put our characters in their own videos. That is the point, but see §8 (coins and impersonators).

### L6. Silent and nearly wordless travels further
- **Evidence:** both are silent, and the remixes and posts about them came in at least 6 languages (§3).
- **Change:** keep the hook pill at 7 words or fewer (hit rule 6, unchanged). Keep Lenny's Drop-ins readable with the sound off; his spoken rant stays for Recreates.

### L7. Expect novelty to fade; build for a second and third view
- **Evidence:** Jean Phil's TikTok engagement fell −90% in 11 days (§2).
- **Change:** none to the plan. It supports the series and the vote (repeat viewers), not chasing one spike.
  - Track both comps' decay once a week with 2 free LC `creator_time_series` calls in the weekly review, as context for our own curves.

### L8. A thumbnail check for Lenny (low)
- **Evidence:** across the whole wave the common factor is one absurd hairstyle plus a moustache plus one fixed outfit.
  - Reginald (the quiff) and Franz (crown, cap, sunglasses, the long body) pass.
  - Lenny (a suit, a tan, the phone) relies on the phone pose.
- **Change:** a 50-ft muted-thumbnail check of Lenny's master next to the other two. **No redesign mid-run** (§4b rule 5); report it to the owner only if he fails.

## 8. Do NOT copy

| Don't | Why | Our rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Coins, tokens or "PFP packs"** | $JEANPHIL fell 99% within one day; $ABU −46% in a day; scam copy contracts; exposés (1.6M views; "part 38" of a series). [I] It is a financial promotion with fraud risk on both platforms | Never (4 Oct playbook). [I] Add a line to each bio: "No coin. No token. Ever." Scammers will launch coins in our characters' names the day one takes off |
| **Posing as a real person** ("Dancer \| Actor \| Comedian"; "he cut his own hair") | "Bamboozled" backlash; 70% of young Europeans would not follow a computer-generated person; Instagram's AI-profile label (from 31 Aug) and EU AI Act Art. 50 | We always label as AI; the odd eyes make it plain fiction |
| **A real culture's or religion's dress as the joke** (Abu: thobe + "habibi"; "selling … is haram habibi" in coin posts, X) | National-origin and religion hate-speech lines on both platforms; it is the obvious temptation for our Borat-type | Borat-type rule C7 (invented country only) stays strict; the thobe template is out of bounds |
