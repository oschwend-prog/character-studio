VIRAL AI CHARACTER LIBRARY — V1

## Core Concept
Create a library of highly recognizable AI characters designed to be dropped into already-viral video clips.
The characters should NOT require original storylines or complex setups. The existing viral clip provides the context,
action and comedic setup.

Our characters add a new layer of comedy through:
- exaggerated personality
- exaggerated appearance
- unexpected reactions
- recognizable archetypes
- recurring visual identity
- recurring mannerisms / catchphrases

**Core formula:** Recognizable viral clip + recognizable character + exaggerated reaction = highly efficient viral content.
The characters should be recognizable within the first 1–2 seconds, ideally before they speak.

## 1. KAREN SUPREME
**Archetype:** The ultimate Karen — entitlement, complaints and confrontation taken to absurd levels.
**Visual:** 40s–50s; immaculate blonde bob; oversized sunglasses; designer athleisure; luxury handbag; perfectly maintained
appearance; permanently unimpressed expression; clipboard / phone / AirPods optional.
**Personality:** believes everyone is incompetent; always wants "the manager"; completely confident that she is right; turns
tiny inconveniences into major crises; treats every environment as if she owns it.
**Comedy:** Drop her into ANY confrontation or chaotic situation. Examples: Dinosaur attack → "I'd like to speak to the
manager." Airport chaos → demands the manager of the airport. Alien invasion → complains about parking. Luxury hotel →
complains about the room. Hell → asks for a refund.
**Signature line:** "I'd like to speak to whoever is in charge."

## 2. BIG DAVE
**Archetype:** The stereotypical British lad who is completely unfazed by everything.
**Visual:** huge / imposing build; 30s–40s; shaved or very short hair; tracksuit / football shirt / polo; pint or takeaway
coffee; ordinary British bloke appearance.
**Personality:** extremely calm; minimal emotional reaction; dry British humour; thinks everything is "a bit much"; never
seems impressed.
**Comedy:** Put him into an absolutely insane situation and have him react like it's mildly inconvenient. Examples: Volcano
erupts → "Bit much." Shark attacks → "Jesus." Alien invasion → "Well, that's new." Car explodes → "That's coming out of my
deposit." Dinosaur appears → "You don't see that every day."
**Signature line:** "Bit much."

## 3. GRANNY GANGSTER
**Archetype:** Sweet-looking grandmother who is secretly the most dangerous person in the room.
**Visual:** 75–85 years old; tiny / elegant; floral dress or cardigan; pearls; handbag; immaculate hair; extremely innocent
appearance; often surrounded by enormous bodyguards.
**Personality:** polite; calm; maternal; terrifyingly authoritative; treats criminal activity as completely normal; never
raises her voice.
**Comedy:** The contrast between her appearance and what she's doing creates the joke. Examples: Arrives at nightclub in
armoured Maybach. Threatens a gangster while holding a handbag. Gives orders to six enormous bodyguards. Walks into a fight
and everyone immediately stops. Gets involved in a street argument.
**Signature line:** "Now, darling..."

## 4. LOLA LUXE
**Archetype:** The absurdly wealthy, completely detached luxury girl.
**Visual:** 20s; extremely glamorous; immaculate hair and makeup; couture / quiet luxury; enormous sunglasses; jewellery;
luxury handbag; private jet / yacht / supercar aesthetic.
**Personality:** completely disconnected from normal life; thinks everything should be luxurious; casually spends absurd
amounts of money; finds ordinary things offensive; has impossible standards.
**Comedy:** Put her into normal or chaotic situations and let her react from the perspective of someone who thinks £100,000
is pocket change. Examples: Business class → "Where's the bedroom?" £5m house → "It's quite small." Normal supermarket →
horrified by prices. Ferrari → asks if it's the cheap model. Hotel suite → complains about the view.
**Signature line:** "This is actually quite embarrassing."

## 5. THE SIGMA
**Archetype:** The completely emotionless, hyper-confident internet "Sigma" character.
**Visual:** male, 20s–30s; extremely sharp jawline; dark hair; black suit / black clothing; sunglasses; perfect physique;
minimalist appearance; almost never smiles.
**Personality:** says almost nothing; completely self-confident; never reacts emotionally; behaves as though nothing matters;
walks away from situations rather than participating.
**Comedy:** The world becomes completely insane around him while he remains totally calm. Examples: Explosion behind him →
adjusts sunglasses. Everyone running → walks slowly. Dinosaur appears → keeps walking. Car crashes → doesn't react. Entire
city collapses → checks his watch.
**Signature behaviour:** No reaction. Walk away.

## 6. CHAD
**Archetype:** The stereotypical hyper-confident "bro".
**Visual:** 20s; extremely muscular / athletic; perfect jawline; backwards baseball cap; tank top; sunglasses; exaggerated
masculine physique.
**Personality:** 100% confidence; 0% self-awareness; thinks he's good at everything; says "bro" constantly; never admits he's
wrong; takes ridiculous risks.
**Comedy:** His confidence is always much greater than his competence. Examples: "Bro, I can definitely land this plane."
Attempts impossible physical challenge. Gives terrible dating advice. Gets into absurd situations because he thinks he can
handle them. Sees danger and says "Easy."
**Signature line:** "Bro, trust me."

## 7. TONY TWO PHONES
**Archetype:** Classic old-school gangster / mob boss exaggerated into a meme.
**Visual:** 40s–60s; expensive Italian suit; slicked-back hair; gold watch; two or three phones; sunglasses; heavy entourage;
luxury black car.
**Personality:** everything is an emergency; constantly negotiating; takes tiny problems extremely seriously; speaks quietly
and intensely; always seems to be conducting some enormous operation.
**Comedy:** Put him into completely mundane situations. Examples: Negotiating over a sandwich. Serious phone call while
ordering coffee. Treating a restaurant reservation like a criminal operation. Sending six bodyguards to retrieve a lost
wallet. Negotiating the price of a parking space.
**Signature line:** "We have a situation."

## 8. PROFESSOR CONSPIRACY
**Archetype:** The guy who believes absolutely everything is connected.
**Visual:** 40s–60s; messy hair; glasses; slightly chaotic clothing; trench coat / old jacket; papers / diagrams / laptop;
obsessive expression.
**Personality:** paranoid; extremely confident; sees patterns everywhere; never accepts the obvious explanation; constantly
"connecting the dots".
**Comedy:** Drop him into normal viral clips and let him invent an insane explanation. Examples: Pigeons → government
surveillance. Rain → weather manipulation. Celebrity appearance → cloning. Restaurant closes → "They're hiding something."
Someone sneezes → "Exactly."
**Signature line:** "Think about it."

## 9. GYM JESUS
**Archetype:** The extreme gym bro who interprets EVERYTHING through fitness.
**Visual:** enormous muscular physique; tiny gym shorts; tank top; veins / exaggerated musculature; shaker bottle; headphones;
permanently intense expression.
**Personality:** obsessed with gains; protein is the answer to everything; sees every situation as a workout; treats ordinary
people as insufficiently disciplined.
**Comedy:** Put him into non-gym situations. Examples: Orders steak and asks for protein macros. Bench presses a car. Goes on
a date and asks about her squat PR. Goes to the beach and starts training. Sees a giant object and asks if it can be used for
deadlifts.
**Signature line:** "No excuses."

## 10. THE USELESS SUPERHERO
**Archetype:** The spectacular superhero who is completely incompetent.
**Visual:** perfect superhero physique; dramatic costume; cape; cinematic lighting; heroic face; looks like the world's
greatest superhero.
**Personality:** extremely confident; completely useless; doesn't understand what's happening; frequently makes situations
worse; believes he's impressive.
**Comedy:** Use spectacular visual situations but give him ridiculous dialogue/reactions. Examples: Arrives at robbery →
robber already gone. Flies into a building → "How do I land?" Saves someone → accidentally destroys their car. Faces giant
monster → asks if anyone has a plan. Dramatic superhero landing → breaks the pavement and hurts himself.
**Signature line:** "Don't worry. I'm here."

## CONTENT PRINCIPLE
These characters should primarily be inserted into existing viral clips, rather than creating expensive original scenes.

The original clip supplies: context; movement; location; action; existing audience familiarity; comedic setup.
The AI character supplies: surprise; exaggeration; new punchline; recognizable personality.

**Production philosophy:** Do NOT overcomplicate the videos. The ideal workflow is: Find viral clip → identify comedic role →
select character → replace/insert character → exaggerate reaction → publish.
The audience should immediately understand: "I know this type of person." Then: "Why the hell is HE/SHE in this video?"
That surprise is the hook.
