# ODD EYES Character Studio

Machine that turns trends into approved, posted 1080p AI-character clips (Biscuit, Reginald)
on TikTok + Instagram Reels, with a terminal to approve, track spend and see results.

## How it works
- Thin agent, thick code. The Python `studio` CLI owns ALL state (Supabase Postgres schema `studio` + Storage) and every deterministic step.
- **All state goes through `bin/studio`.** Never write to the DB or Storage any other way. Run it as `bin/studio <cmd>`.
- Agent/skills only do MCP + judgement work (Higgsfield, vidIQ, visual QA, captions) and call the CLI.
- Dev: `uv run pytest -q`. Python 3.12 (uv), typer, psycopg 3, pydantic 2, ffmpeg 8 (no drawtext: overlays are PIL PNGs).

- Master: 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264 High, ~15 Mbps, AAC 320k 48 kHz, -14 LUFS +/-1, true peak <= -1 dBFS, 6-16 s (owner 2026-10-05, prefers longer: classics (tier iconic) 12-15 s, other clips 8-10 s, 16 s the hard max; eye loops 6-8 s). A silent master (`music: in_app`, the owner adds the song in the Instagram app) skips the loudness rule for silence only.
- ODD EYES: character's RIGHT eye (viewer's left) ice-blue, LEFT eye (viewer's right) amber; masters end on the dance: no eye close-up, glint or sting (owner 2026-10-05; `closeup: null` in the master spec), the odd eyes show in the dance itself and in the two-dot bug; two-dot bug top-right at (972,268) r=15, `#8FD3FF` / `#FFB040`.
- Viral modes: `dropin` and `recreate`. **Drop-in is the default for every video** (owner decision 2026-10-05); Recreate only when there is no usable clip. A Drop-in source has `has_watermark=false` (no watermark or other creator's handle), `has_overlay=false` (no burned-in text) and `kind != 'synthetic'`. Other people in the background and famous clips are allowed (`other_people` is recorded, no longer a gate); the real star is always replaced by our character. **Children are fine in a clip** (owner 2026-10-06): `has_minors` is recorded only, but the star we replace must be an adult (a drop blocks on `star.child`; like for like: an adult character cannot take a child's place); a child as the star or main subject and sexualised content stay rejected. Account `dropin_share` is 1.00 (= no cap); the IG guard still cuts it to 0.20.
- Clip sources: Higgsfield Genjutsu gallery clips (`higgsfield_library`, `preset_id`, filed only as backup picks when the day's Instagram + TikTok search (one a weekday, owner 2026-10-05) could not run or found fewer than 2 usable clips), clips the owner attaches on the terminal's Make-it sheet (`owner_inbox`, `studio source ingest-owner`), or a direct media link imported through `media_import_url`. A real pick with no usable clip is made automatically as a Recreate; production never waits on a download and never asks the owner for a clip.
- Drop-in playbook (full text in `.claude/skills/daily-run/SKILL.md`): swap-friendly clips (one clear performer, full body, static camera; Genjutsu gallery first); trim to the best window (classics 12-14 s, other clips 7-9 s) with `bin/studio source trim` before generating (Genjutsu is paid per second); transform (hook in the first second, caption in the character's voice, his part and the owner's gadgets); credit the original creator, never keep their watermark or handle; music `original` by default.
- Cost model (`studio.planning.estimate_credits`, one source of truth): Recreate 160; Drop-in `ceil(seconds x 11) + 3`, +30 for an `ai_beat` (91 for 8 s). `bin/studio plan estimate` prices the actual trimmed seconds.
- Music (`features.music`, `proposal.owner_music`): `original` (default for a Drop-in: the clip's own audio via the Genjutsu output, muxed back with `bin/studio master mux-audio` if lost), `in_app` (silent master; fallback when Instagram mutes a chart song; never posted on autopilot), `ai_beat` (Seedance render, +30; a Recreate's default).
- Never: scrape/download from TikTok/Instagram on the automation's own initiative (no crawling, no bulk grabbing); the main source is the owner's own saved TikTok/Instagram clips, dropped on the terminal as a file or a link (owner 2026-10-06; `bin/studio source fetch --pick <id>` and the cloud drop job take ONE dropped or approved pick's public clip with yt-dlp, no login, no cookies; `source purge` deletes it after posting); ADD third-party audio we sourced ourselves (owner amendment 2026-10-05: Drop-ins keep the original clip audio by default, i.e. the audio the clip already carries; nothing else third-party goes in); post without the AI label; post more than 2x per account per day; spend past the monthly cap (default 6,000 Higgsfield credits).
- Slots (Europe/London, `zoneinfo`, never naive datetimes): Biscuit 19:00, Reginald 19:30; weeks 1-2 Tue/Wed/Thu, from week 3 Mon-Fri.
- KPI bars are binding and must not be renegotiated after seeing data (see plan header).
