# ODD EYES Character Studio: context for the hook skill

The `viral-hook-creator` skill reads this file (its step 2). Everything here overrides the skill's defaults
(X/Twitter, business audience, 120 characters).

## What we make
- Short vertical videos (6-16 s) on **Instagram Reels** now, **TikTok** later. An AI character replaces the star of a
  viral clip (Drop-in) and keeps the clip's original sound. The account is openly AI (label on every post).
- The **hook is the on-screen pill** in the first second: it must land with the sound off, in one glance.
- Audience: Gen Z and millennial entertainment viewers, UK and US, scrolling fast. Goal: watch time, shares, follows.

## Hook rules (binding)
- At most **42 characters**, one or two short lines, no hashtags, no emoji in the pill.
- True to what happens in the clip; never explains the joke; a different angle for each candidate.
- In the character's voice (below). Read `characters/<slug>/bible.md` (its `## Voice (captions)` and the newest owner note
  at the top win over anything older) before writing.
- When `docs/research/2026-10-07-hit-patterns.md` exists, apply its hook rules: they come from current hits.
- Never: a real person's name or likeness, a brand, a nationality joke, anything about a child, mocking disability.
- Output: 3 candidates, each with its pattern name, then the one you recommend and why (one line).

## The characters (live roster)
- **Franz** (cream long-haired mini dachshund): the **happy show-off** (owner 2026-10-07; no longer posh, no crown).
  Proud, upbeat, first person, short bouncy lines; catchphrase "Sausage coming through!"; move: the Wiggle.
  Never barks, no baby talk, no posh talk.
- **Reginald** (deadpan English butler, mid-40s): never speaks and never breaks character. His hook reads like a calm
  label or a butler's understatement; it may name the dance, his lines never do. Catchphrase "As you were."
- **Lenny Gold** (manic Hollywood agent, black slim suit, phone glued to his ear): agent-speak in short bursts, then smug
  calm. Catchphrase "You're welcome."; move: the Tie Snap.
